# Facts — "रेमिट्यान्स: भित्र के हुन्छ?" (भाग २)
Prepared 2026-11-14. Public sources only. No internal bank process or data used.

## Rules (Level 1 — NRB)
Source A: Nepal Rastra Bank Remittance Bylaws, 2023 (approved by NRB Board 2023-01-17; English translation)
https://nrb.org.np/contents/uploads/2019/12/NRB-Remittance-Bylaws-2023_-English.pdf
- Inward remittance only by entities licensed by NRB's Foreign Exchange Management Department (Bylaw 3(1)); BFIs also need a licence (Bylaw 8). Licence valid 5 years.
- Agreement with a foreign firm/company needs prior NRB approval (Bylaw 12(1)); transactions start only after approval (12(4)).
- Pre-funding: company "shall have to bring foreign currency into Nepal from abroad as pre-funding via banking system" (9(a)). If paying before funding, must hold an unconditional foreign-bank guarantee or cash deposit for the full amount (9(b)).
- Payment directly or through sub-agents (Bylaw 9, 35); payment through licensed PSP/PSO allowed but "can not be made using cash" (9(p)).
- Only the published service charge may be taken (9(n)).
- Outward remittance not allowed for remittance companies (10(1)(a)).
- Amounts recorded in foreign currency and NPR (Schedule-18). Exchange rate source NOT specified → video says only "सटही दरमा".
- NOT in source: beneficiary ID rules, payout time limit, cash limits → not stated in video. "कारोबार नम्बर" given to sender = general industry practice (described generically).

Source B: NRB FXMD, "Licensed entities by NRB for FX transactions as on Asar end 2083 (16 July 2026)"
https://www.nrb.org.np/fxm/licensed-entities-by-nrb-for-fx-transactions-as-on-asar-end-2083-july-16-2026/

## Hundi
Source D: Onlinekhabar (पुस १, २०८२): Nepali embassies in South Korea and UAE asked Nepalis not to send money via hundi; embassies report such cases to the government, linked to AML/CFT.
- Video says only: hundi is the informal route outside the banking system; embassies have asked not to use it. No claim about penalties.

## Link to banking system (explanation, not opinion)
- Formal route brings foreign currency into Nepal through the banking system (Source A, 9(a)). Hundi does not use that route.
